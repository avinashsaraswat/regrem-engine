# Changelog

## 0.7.1, 2026-11-12

This release changes finding/finding.py, so every finding's fingerprint changes and a finding signed on 0.7.0 asks for a fresh signature. The pasted screens and fingerprints are rerun on 0.7.1.

- The tool's printed lines say institution where they said bank: the warning that opens every screen, the commitment table's heading, the line on who closes a self-identified issue, the deny-list count and two refusals. The advice on renaming an evidence file now says to keep the file's own name after the id. Its comments follow. The pages have said financial institution since a commit made after 0.7.0, and the front page's Status line now does too. The invented cases keep their invented banks.
- Since 0.7.0, docs/ holds six usage guides: for the finding owner, internal audit, the weekly run, the engineer's first week, measuring the work and how the tool works, which has three diagrams. The front page opens with what the tool does, with five pictures for readers new to remediation.

## 0.7.0, first public release

The first public release of RegRem Engine. It holds the finding tool with its document reader, two invented findings and a sample PDF. It also holds the tie-out engine with its invented matter, 21 invented library cases and the drafting procedures for an AI assistant. A page in docs/ sets out, as a concept, the three controls a forward-deployed team delivering the kit would need. RELEASE.json holds the hash of every file a finding's page depends on and of every test asset.
