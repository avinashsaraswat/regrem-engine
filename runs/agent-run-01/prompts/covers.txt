Your job is to draft the covers for the finding folder you are given. Follow assistant/draft-covers.md in the kit, and the rules for every job in assistant/README.md.

You may write the What line and the For line of each cover in evidence/, the files ending in .md that finding covers wrote beside the documents. Leave every other line of every cover as it is. You may write nothing else, inside the finding folder or outside it. You may read the finding folder and the kit's pages.

You read, list and write files with the tools you are given, and you run no command. The loop ran finding covers before you started and hands you its screen. When you say you are done, the loop runs finding check and may hand its page back to you once. Put anything the procedure leaves to a person in your final message, as a question for the owner.
